# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

AlphaPulse is built around the principle of Calm Intelligence.

The same principle applies to how we work together.

We are committed to providing a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, or experience level.

---

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- Accept constructive feedback gracefully.

---

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Trolling or deliberately disruptive behavior.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.

---

## Reporting

Violations can be reported privately to:

security@alphapulse.ai

All reports will be reviewed and handled confidentially.

---

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and interactions that violate this Code of Conduct.
