## Direct Competitors

### Exploding Topics

Website:
https://explodingtopics.com

Strengths:
- Strong trend detection
- Large content engine
- SEO dominance

Weaknesses:
- Limited AI-specific coverage
- Low explainability
- Generalized signals

Opportunity:
AlphaPulse provides AI-native intelligence with transparent evidence.

---

### Crunchbase

Website:
https://www.crunchbase.com

Strengths:
- Company database
- Funding data
- Market penetration

Weaknesses:
- Reactive data
- Late-stage visibility
- Limited early discovery

Opportunity:
AlphaPulse focuses on pre-funding discovery.

---

### Product Hunt

Website:
https://www.producthunt.com

Strengths:
- Product discovery
- Community engagement

Weaknesses:
- Launch-day bias
- Popularity-driven

Opportunity:
AlphaPulse tracks adoption before launch visibility.

---

### CB Insights

Website:
https://www.cbinsights.com

Strengths:
- Enterprise intelligence
- Deep research

Weaknesses:
- Expensive
- Complex
- Not founder-friendly

Opportunity:
Affordable intelligence for individuals.
