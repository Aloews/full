# AlphaPulse Pricing Strategy

Version: 1.0

Status: Approved Draft

---

## Objective

Create sustainable recurring revenue while maintaining accessibility.

---

## Pricing Philosophy

Users pay for:

- saved time
- better decisions
- signal quality

Users do not pay for:

- raw data
- dashboards
- complexity

---

# Tier 1

Free

Price:

$0

Includes:

- Daily Brief
- Trending Projects
- Limited Dashboard
- Basic Search

Limitations:

- No watchlists
- No history
- No premium reports

---

# Tier 2

Pro

$19/month

- Unlimited Signals
- Watchlists

- Advanced Search
