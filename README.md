# AlphaPulse

Calm Intelligence for Emerging AI Companies.

AlphaPulse helps founders, investors, operators, and researchers discover promising AI companies before they become widely known.

Instead of overwhelming users with noise, AlphaPulse delivers curated signals, structured analysis, and daily intelligence in under five minutes.

---

## Vision

The AI ecosystem is expanding faster than anyone can track.

Thousands of new projects launch every month across:

- GitHub
- Reddit
- Hacker News
- Developer Communities

Most people discover important companies too late.

AlphaPulse continuously monitors the ecosystem and surfaces the most interesting opportunities using explainable scoring and AI-assisted analysis.

---

## Core Principles

### Calm Intelligence

Information should reduce anxiety, not increase it.

### Explainability

Every signal must be traceable to evidence.

### Quality Over Quantity

Five useful insights are better than fifty noisy alerts.

### Human-Centered AI

AI supports analysis.
AI does not replace judgment.
